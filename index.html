How PSS works

LEVEL 1 · Six teams play a single round-robin league. Every team faces each opponent once (5 matches each).

LEVEL 2 · The top two qualify for one Grand Final. If a qualifying position remains tied after all tie-breakers, the organizer arranges a tie-breaker match.

Level 1 · League fixtures

All 15 fixtures are listed from the start. The organizer changes match status and enters scores.
